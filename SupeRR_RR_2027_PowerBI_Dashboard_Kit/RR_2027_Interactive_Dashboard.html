Decision guardrails

Availability: final 2027 retention, auction listing, price and auction-purse data were not provided. Treat every player as hypothetical unless formally confirmed available.
Overseas: compare each overseas recruit against the four-overseas-player XI limit.
Model: the pressure index excludes exact required run rate; scoring extras are incomplete; selection score is heuristic and not a forecast probability.
Data: six SupeRR competition files, Phase 4 reliability shortlist; RR 2026 roster: Rajasthan Royals / Cricbuzz; champions: official IPL winner records. Documentation and source URLs included in package.
